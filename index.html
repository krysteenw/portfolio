01About

I'm a Computer Science graduate and full-stack developer working at the intersection of software engineering and operations. By day, I support application performance and incident response for Walmart's Corporate Security Operations Center — monitoring a 5,800+ device network and a 98-node fire alarm system, correlating alerts, and driving issues to permanent root-cause resolution. Outside of that, I design and ship full-stack applications: React and Node services, a Flutter mobile app with an offline-first multi-database architecture, and identity & access management controls built on Microsoft Entra ID and AWS IAM.

I bring that same discipline to code: clear root-cause analysis, secure-by-default design, and calm, well-documented incident leadership.
